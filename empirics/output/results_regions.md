# Who is left behind: regional and country disaggregation

Pre = 2019-2021 average, post = 2023-2024 average. ODA received is from OECD DAC2A (all official donors, constant USD); recipient-level data end in 2024, so exposure to the 2025 collapse is computed as each recipient's 2024 US share times the observed real US cut (57%). 'Market access' = any PPG or PNG bond issuance, or positive net private debt flows, in 2022-24 (World Bank IDS). 'Left behind' = net ODA >= 5% of GNI and no market access.
